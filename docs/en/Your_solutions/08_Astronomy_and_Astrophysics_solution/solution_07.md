## Problem 7: Megastructures

### Given / Knowns
* Mass of Mercury (available building material), $M = 3.3 \times 10^{23} \text{ kg}$
* Surface density of the solar panels, $\sigma = 10 \text{ kg/m}^2$

### Concepts & Assumptions
* **Surface Density:** Surface density is defined as mass per unit area. Therefore, the total mass used to build the sphere is equal to the surface density multiplied by the total surface area of the sphere.
* **Geometry:** A Dyson Sphere is assumed to be a perfect, unbroken geometric sphere encompassing the star.
* **Material Utilization:** We assume 100% of Mercury's mass is perfectly converted into these solar panels with no waste.

### Formulas
* Surface Density: $\sigma = \frac{M}{A}$
* Total Mass: $M = \sigma A$
* Surface Area of a Sphere: $A = 4\pi R^2$
* Combined Equation: $M = \sigma (4\pi R^2)$

### Step-by-Step Derivation

First, isolate the surface area ($A$) in the density equation to find out how much total panel area we can build:
$$A = \frac{M}{\sigma}$$
$$A = \frac{3.3 \times 10^{23} \text{ kg}}{10 \text{ kg/m}^2}$$
$$A = 3.3 \times 10^{22} \text{ m}^2$$

Now, substitute this total area into the formula for the surface area of a sphere to solve for the radius ($R$):
$$A = 4\pi R^2$$
$$3.3 \times 10^{22} \text{ m}^2 = 4\pi R^2$$

Divide both sides by $4\pi$:
$$R^2 = \frac{3.3 \times 10^{22}}{4\pi}$$
$$R^2 \approx \frac{3.3 \times 10^{22}}{12.566}$$
$$R^2 \approx 2.626 \times 10^{21} \text{ m}^2$$

Take the square root to find $R$:
$$R = \sqrt{2.626 \times 10^{21} \text{ m}^2}$$
$$R \approx 5.124 \times 10^{10} \text{ m}$$

Convert the final answer into kilometers for better context:
$$R \approx 51,240,000 \text{ km}$$
*(Note: This is roughly 51.2 million kilometers, which is about one-third of the distance from the Earth to the Sun!)*

### Final Answer
The radius of the Dyson sphere would be approximately **$5.12 \times 10^{10}$ meters**, or **51.24 million km**.
